2. **Structural validity (VERIFIED_YEARS):** for each year's table:
   - first bracket `min === 0`;
   - brackets contiguous and non-overlapping: `bracket[i+1].min === bracket[i].max + 1`;

   - `fuelCostPerKm` and `maintenanceCostPerKm` strictly between 0.1 and 10 (rand/km — catches unconverted cents/km, which would be 40-260, and zero placeholders).
3. **Distinctness / anti-copy-paste (VERIFIED_YEARS only):** pairwise `expect(packA.travelDeemedCostTable).not.toEqual(packB.travelDeemedCostTable)` for every pair among 2025/2026/2027.
4. **Year-anchored spot checks** (pins the tables to the official PAYE-GEN-01-G03-A01 values so a wholesale wrong-year paste fails even if structurally valid):
   - 2025: first bracket `fixedCostAnnual === 34480`; last bracket `{ fuelCostPerKm: 2.512, maintenanceCostPerKm: 1.189 }`.
   - 2026: first bracket `fixedCostAnnual === 33940`; last bracket `{ fuelCostPerKm: 2.429, maintenanceCostPerKm: 1.225 }`.
   - 2027: first bracket `{ max: 115000, fixedCostAnnual: 38344 }` (R115k boundaries — structurally different year); table has exactly 9 brackets with second-to-last `max === 920000`; last bracket `maintenanceCostPerKm === 1.269`.
5. **2027 Budget-2026 completeness:** `getIndividualTaxRulePack(2027)` has `retirement.annualCap === 430000`, `cgt.annualExclusion === 50000`, `cgt.primaryResidenceExclusion === 3000000`, `cgt.deathExclusion === 440000`.
6. **Provisional tax values (VERIFIED_YEARS):** `safeHarbourTaxableIncomeThreshold === 1000000`, `safeHarbourBasicAmountOrActualPctBelowThreshold === 0.90`, `safeHarbourActualPctAboveThreshold === 0.80`, `underestimationPenaltyRate === 0.20`.
7. **2024 structural safety only:** `getIndividualTaxRulePack(2024).travelDeemedCostTable.length > 0` and `provisionalTax` defined. No value or distinctness assertions for 2024 (its data is a flagged out-of-scope carry-over from 2025).
